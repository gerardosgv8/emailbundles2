Which email production path is best for each use case?. Choose Klaviyo drag-and-drop for ESP-only teams, custom HTML for engineering-owned systems, and Mailcraft Studio when you need portable production HTML with pack-wide branding and structured campaign fills.

Best for: Klaviyo-only lifecycle marketers

Pros: fastest path if every send stays inside Klaviyo; native personalization and flows. Cons: weaker portability if you later move to Mailchimp, Salesforce Marketing Cloud, or a hybrid stack. Use when: one ESP is permanent and designers are comfortable in the canvas.

Best for: custom HTML kit maintainers

Pros: maximum control over table structure, VML, and ESP paste. Cons: brand and footer updates multiply across every template file. Use when: engineering owns markup and marketers rarely touch HTML.

Best for: Mailcraft Studio Email Marketing Starter Kit

Pros: own the HTML; Brand Wizard stamps logo/colors/buttons/footer across the pack; Content Wizard fills one send without hunting nested cells. Cons: not an ESP. You still paste into Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or SFMC and wire merge tags. Use when: agencies and ecommerce teams need consistent branding across welcome, cart, launch, promo, and newsletter layouts.

What are the pros and cons of Mailcraft Studio versus building only in Klaviyo?. Mailcraft Studio optimizes the pre-ESP production layer (portable HTML and brand consistency), while Klaviyo optimizes in-ESP orchestration. Many teams use both: brand and fill in Mailcraft, then personalize and send in Klaviyo.
