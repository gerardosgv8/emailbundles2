Mailcraft Studio User Guide

The Mailcraft Studio User Guide explains how to brand the Email Marketing Starter Kit with Brand Wizard, fill campaign copy with Content Wizard, and paste ESP-ready HTML into Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or Salesforce Marketing Cloud.

How do I get started with the Email Marketing Starter Kit?

  1. Download and extract the Email Marketing Starter Kit zip.
  2. Use Brand Wizard to set logo, colors, buttons, and footer, then apply brand across the zip.
  3. Use Content Wizard to fill headlines, body, CTAs, and images for one layout.
  4. Paste filled HTML into your ESP, wire merge tags, and send a test.
